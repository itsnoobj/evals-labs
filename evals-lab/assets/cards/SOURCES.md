# Card images: sources

Four card images, all fetched on 2026-11-20. Each is official issuer artwork, shown for identification in a talk demo; owner: the bank. Trademarks and card designs belong to the issuer and the card networks. Remove an image on the issuer's request.

Processing, same for all four: cropped to the card edge, resized to at most 480 px wide, saved as WebP (quality 88). No colours or text were changed. For IDFC First Power+ the card corners were rounded with a transparent mask because the source shows the card on a grey background.
