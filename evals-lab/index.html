Details

About 10 minutes. Nothing leaves your browser. Let us find out, and learn how teams measure an AI agent.

What is an eval?A test for an AI: you ask a question you know the answer to, then check what it said. Unlike a normal test, the AI can answer differently each time.

What is an agent?An AI that can use tools, such as a calculator or a lookup. Here it has two tools and one job: pick the best card for a purchase.

    2 · Break it and see what notices

    Loading the checks.

    Which check reads what
    How the LLM judge works
    Word meanings

    The "tools" checks look at which tools the agent called and in what order. "Numbers come from tools" means no number was made up. The LLM judge is a second AI that reads the question, the tool results and the reply, then gives a 0 to 5 grade. It stays NOT RUN until you ask for it. We ask five times because one grade can be a fluke.

    Why five runs?

    An AI can pass once and fail the next time. Asking each question several times shows which rows are solid, mixed or failing. A fixed list of questions with known answers is also called a golden set.

    Five runs per question is a rough guide, good to about 10 points either way.

    4 · Errors add up

    Where the curve comes from

    If each step is right 90% of the time, two steps in a row are both right 0.9 × 0.9 = 81% of the time, three steps 73%, and so on. Letting each step try again helps, but only if something notices that a step went wrong.

    One weak step is small. Ten in a row add up.
